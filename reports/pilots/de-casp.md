# Pilot plan: Rheinwerk Digital (fictional)

_Generated by `ocp report` from the discovery notes in `config/` and the simulated pilot. Do not edit by hand._

**Who:** crypto exchange, licensed CASP under MiCA, Germany (BaFin), about 2,400 customers. **Team:** 3 analysts, 300 minutes a day each on alerts, office hours 8:00 to 18:00.

**Risk appetite:** prohibited sanctions, ransomware, darknet_market; high risk mixer, scam, stolen_funds; restricted gambling, no_kyc_exchange; indirect exposure up to 3 hops.

## Rules compiled from discovery (before tuning on the shared history)

| Rule | Fires on | Threshold | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| prohibited-direct | any transfer with sanctions, ransomware, darknet_market | any amount | critical |
| prohibited-indirect | funds from sanctions, ransomware, darknet_market within 3 hops | share >= 1.0% | critical |
| high-risk-direct | any transfer with mixer, scam, stolen_funds | retail >= EUR 0, hnw >= EUR 0, business >= EUR 0 | high |
| high-risk-indirect | funds from mixer, scam, stolen_funds within 2 hops | share >= 5% | high |
| rescreen | a past counterparty is attributed later | within 30 days | as the rule it would have fired |
| restricted-24h | sent to gambling, no_kyc_exchange in 24 h | total >= EUR 5,000 | medium |
| structuring-7d | unhosted withdrawals at 60-100% of EUR 1,000 in 7 days | count >= 4 | high |
| escalating-payments | rising payments to one counterparty the customer had not paid in the 30 days before | 3 in 14 days, total >= EUR 1,000 | high |
| pass-through | deposit with high-risk or prohibited exposure forwarded within 24 h | forwarded >= 70% | high |

## Integration

Alerts arrive by signed webhook, are de-duplicated by event id and applied in version order, and open one ticket each in case management. Each alert gets an evidence pack and a drafted disposition that cites it; low-risk alerts outside the hard rules go to a fast lane with 10% sampled for full review; sanctions, ransomware and darknet exposure, behavioural alerts and amounts of EUR 10,000 or more always get a full human review.
